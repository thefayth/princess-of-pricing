# Savings Kingdom Design Brief

Audience: US invite-only users aligning existing purchases/subscriptions with better prices and owned rewards.

Princess of Pricing stars Faith's public-approved illustrated avatar. Users choose Royal Atlas, Rose Ledger, or Emerald Arcade. Royal Atlas leads with the illustrated Kingdom, Rose Ledger with a points register, and Emerald Arcade with a compact next-action band and Palace progress. All consume the same dashboard and preserve live HTML names, amounts, counts and deadlines.

Desktop uses a compact navigation rail, readable map and action panel. Mobile prioritizes the next action, district chapters and quests. Operational views prioritize scanning, filters, forms and review. Teal actions, green confirmed value, amber deadlines and restrained red alerts support clarity.

Use the existing React/Vinext shared components and Lucide icons. Persist only theme and avatar visibility preferences on device; respect reduced motion. Preserve originals and maintain asset provenance/license metadata. Faith Presence governs authorship/asset boundaries; Faith Visual Design governs target/render quality; Faith-Aware Development governs agency, accessibility, privacy and dignified representation. No voice synthesis is authorized. Paid plans and disclosed referrals are the approved business model; prices remain proposed and billing inactive.

Validate 1440x900, 1024x768 and 390x844, keyboard access, focus, screen readers, empty/offline states and actual interactions. Do not encourage spending for points or present estimates as cash already saved.
